# ChannelForge Alpha 4.5.7

- Fire TV and Android keep the screen awake during active movie and live TV playback. Normal screen timeouts resume when playback is paused or left.
- Fixed an in-app update check that could reject an official APK on Fire TV. If a previous build shows a signing-key error, install the official APK over the existing app once to get the fixed updater and keep your saved data.
