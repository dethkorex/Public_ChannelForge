# ChannelForge Alpha 4.0

ChannelForge is free community TV. Watch channels made by other creators, browse their shared movie libraries in Rewind Cinema, or build your own channel with ChannelForge Studio for Windows and Linux.

## For viewers

- **A new TV guide and menus.** See the current program and its artwork at a glance. Browse with a keyboard, TV remote, or touchscreen on supported devices.
- **A redesigned Rewind Cinema.** Browse a front-facing VHS shelf, select a tape to see the movie details, then choose **Insert Movie**. When you eject a movie, **Continue Watching** saves your place. Removing a movie from the list clears its saved position.
- **Live TV up to 720p.** Picture quality adjusts to the source and connection. Older shows keep their original proportions. On desktop, the channel plays more quietly when you return to the guide.
- **Better playback recovery.** Live TV and Rewind can retry if a Studio's address changes. Movies resume near where they stopped. The desktop live player closes when you leave the guide.
- **A layout for Android phones.** The viewer adapts to portrait and landscape, with touch controls for tuning channels and starting movies. The same public Android package supports remote navigation on Android TV, onn. and Fire TV.

## For channel creators

- **Channel Creator is now ChannelForge Studio.** The redesigned library picker fits more titles on wide screens. Search by title or available library metadata, then choose what goes into your schedule.
- **Use more than one library.** Build a channel from local folders, Plex and Jellyfin in the same Studio. Multiple libraries and connections can contribute to the same lineup.
- **One live broadcast per active channel.** The first viewer starts the broadcast at the current point in the schedule. Other viewers share it. After the last viewer leaves, encoding stops after a short grace period. The schedule keeps moving.
- **Relay support.** A compatible HTTPS relay can serve the same video segments to multiple viewers, reducing repeated transfers from Studio. For the configured Studio, the viewer tries the VPS first and falls back to Tailscale if the stream fails. Other Studios can continue using their own compatible publishing routes.
- **Playback and broadcast diagnostics.** Check playback details in TV and monitor broadcast activity in Studio to help diagnose slow starts and interruptions.

## Updates and availability

- **Cleaner desktop updates.** Windows and Linux packages include TV and Studio with separate launcher icons. Desktop updates preserve your libraries, channels and personal settings.
- **Updates for your device.** Alpha 4.0 is available for Windows, Linux, Fire TV, Android phones, Android TV, and onn. Use the app’s update controls or download the package for your device below.

This is a public alpha. Roku Store availability is on hold for device testing, signing, and certification. If playback stalls on your network, share your device, app version, and whether you were watching live TV or Rewind Cinema in the [ChannelForge Discord](https://discord.gg/Kug9rZQP2D).

Private testers have separate builds with expanded diagnostics, review-and-send reports, and optional crash reporting.
