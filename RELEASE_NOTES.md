# ChannelForge Alpha 4.5.8

- In Studio, Choose Titles now leads to a full-size Arrange Lineup page. Search selected titles, drag the artwork cards, or enter a position to move a title directly.
- Smart Franchise Timeline uses original movie release dates and episode air dates to place films among episodes. Preview the timeline before saving. On an existing franchise channel, choose Restore preset order on save to apply the updated preset.
- Ten new Up Next packages add distinct graphics and layouts, including Christmas and Halloween. Six new original ambience tracks include a Christmas bell jingle and a Halloween soundscape. Each package has a matching ambience option.
- Fire TV and Android carry forward 4.5.7's movie screen-awake and signed updater fixes. If an older official Fire TV build rejects the in-app update, install the [official APK](https://github.com/dethkorex/Public_ChannelForge/releases/download/v4.5.8/ChannelForgeTV-FireTV.apk) over your existing copy to preserve your viewing history.
