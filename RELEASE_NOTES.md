# ChannelForge Alpha 4.6

- Studio’s Watch TV guide and ChannelForge TV show only scheduled shows and movies. Up Next branding cards still play between programs.
- Choose a timeline in Arrange Lineup to see the cards move into scheduled order immediately. When a release-date timeline places a movie between episodes, the lineup shows it between the corresponding episode groups.
- Moving a card by hand applies the exact order when you save. The guide updates to match the saved lineup.
- Up Next cards use the latest saved lineup and branding. A prepared card for the previous lineup is rebuilt before reuse.
- Includes the live TV and Rewind keep-awake fixes from Alpha 4.5.9.
